Threat model

Three classic CAN attacks

DoS flood

Highest-priority ID 0x000 every 1 ms wins arbitration and starves real ECUs.

0x000 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
0x000 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
0x000 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00

CRITICAL

Fuzzing

Random IDs with random payloads probe for crashes and hidden functions.

0x5DA E4 2B 82 2C 69 37 BD 4D
0x661 03 EE 91 62 68 53 C3 0E
0x2BD 9E 04 F4 56 07 A7 4E F9
0x0DC 5C 8F 1A 40 D3 B8 84 7C
HIGH

Impersonation

Forged frames on a legitimate ID, e.g. a max-torque command as the motor inverter.

0x0B4 29 04 0E 74 10 30 00 B1
0x0B4 2E E0 3A 98 10 30 7F 31 ← forged
0x0B4 29 05 0E 76 10 40 00 B4
